# Third-party colour data

The bundled bead palettes under `qr_bead_blueprint/palettes/` are derived from
[`HansBug/pindou-color-data`](https://github.com/HansBug/pindou-color-data),
copyright (c) 2026 HansBug and distributed under the MIT License.

Only the colour code and opaque RGB/HEX fields needed for deterministic offline
matching are retained. The upstream repository documents the original public
and official chart sources and the limitations of screen colours versus physical
beads.
